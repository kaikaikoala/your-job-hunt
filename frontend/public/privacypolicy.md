# Privacy Policy
**Last updated: April 21, 2026**

## 1. Data Ownership

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We store your data solely to make the service work. This includes information related to:
* **Account Access:** Authentication via your Google account.
* **Application Tracking:** Data provided for tracking job applications and refining resumes.
* **Email Synchronization:** If you choose to enable email syncing, you may grant the service access to your Gmail via the Gmail API. We will encrypt and store your Gmail tokens to parse your emails specifically for job-related updates.


## 3. Data Sharing and Advertising
* We do not sell your data.
* We do not share your data with third parties for advertising.
* We do not use your data for any purpose beyond running **Your Job Hunt**.

## 4. Data Deletion
You can request the deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
